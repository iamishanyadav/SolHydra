SOLHYDRA — VIDEO ASSETS FOLDER

Place your local .mp4 background video files here.

The home page hero section (index.html) is pre-configured to look for:
  videos/solhydra-hero.mp4

Format specifications:
- Container: MP4 (H.264 / AVC or H.265 / HEVC)
- Dimensions: 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440 (1440p)
- Optimization: Web-optimized / Fast Start (moov atom at front)
- Audio: The video hero container is automatically muted to comply with browser autoplay policies.

If no video is present, index.html automatically displays the high-tech animated neon cyan/green gradient and the poster image (images/main_truck.png).
