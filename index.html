THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Why Current Clean Trucks Fall Short

Commercial logistics companies want to stop burning diesel. But today's clean truck options force a difficult compromise between deadweight batteries and dangerous high-pressure gas.

PROBLEM 01

Battery Trucks Are Too Heavy

Electric semi-trucks need enormous batteries weighing 8,000 to 10,000 kg. Every kilogram of battery weight takes away payload capacity that a truck could have carried for profit. They also take hours to recharge at special megawatt stations.

PROBLEM 02

Standard Hydrogen is Risky & Costly

Traditional hydrogen fuel-cell trucks compress hydrogen to 700 times normal air pressure (10,000 PSI). These tanks are expensive, carry severe explosion hazards in traffic collisions, and depend on rare hydrogen refueling stations.

SOLHYDRA SOLUTION

Generate Fuel On The Road

Instead of carrying explosive high-pressure gas, SolHydra carries ordinary water. Onboard solar power and battery electricity split water into hydrogen in real-time, feeding the fuel cell immediately as the truck drives.

SIMPLE & RELIABLE PROCESS

How SolHydra Works in 4 Steps

A closed-loop power system designed to keep commercial freight moving smoothly and cleanly.

1

Solar Capture

Durable solar panels on the roof of the truck and trailer continuously absorb sunlight while driving and during loading stops.

2

Battery Buffer

A right-sized, lightweight onboard battery stores solar energy and captures power whenever the driver applies the regenerative brakes.

3

Water Electrolyzer

Battery power runs an onboard water electrolyzer. It splits regular water into clean hydrogen gas on demand at safe, atmospheric pressure.

4

Direct Fuel Cell

The hydrogen flows straight into the fuel cell to generate steady electric power for the motor. Zero heavy storage tanks needed.
