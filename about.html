MISSION & FOUNDATION

Decarbonizing Heavy Freight With Practical Science

Founded by Ishan Yadav, SolHydra was built on a simple question: Why force commercial fleets to choose between deadweight batteries and explosive 700-bar gas cylinders when they can synthesize clean fuel right on the road?

EXECUTION & TIMELINE

Project Development Roadmap

A clear progression from initial concept and modeling to pilot demonstrations on commercial roads.

STAGE 1 • COMPLETED

Concept & Modeling

Thermodynamic mass balance simulations, solar yield calculations, component selection, and preliminary packaging CAD models completed.

STAGE 2 • CURRENT

Bench Test Rig

Testing low-pressure water electrolysis coupled directly to a fuel cell stack on a laboratory bench with automated telemetry control.

STAGE 3 • UPCOMING

Chassis Prototype

Integrating the solar matrix, buffer battery, and water electrolyzer into a physical vehicle chassis for closed-circuit driving trials.

STAGE 4 • PLANNED

Commercial Fleet Pilot

Partnering with commercial logistics companies to validate daily fuel savings, cargo performance, and uptime in real-world freight routes.
