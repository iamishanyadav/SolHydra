QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding safety, water requirements, cold weather driving, and fleet advantages.

Standard hydrogen fuel cell trucks carry large tanks pressurized to 700 bar (10,000 PSI), which pose severe explosion risks during highway accidents. SolHydra completely eliminates this dangerous tank. Hydrogen is synthesized on demand at safe atmospheric pressure (below 3 bar) and consumed immediately by the fuel cell.
When fuel cells operate, they generate mild heat and warm water as a natural byproduct. SolHydra channels this gentle waste heat through an internal coil inside the water tank while driving, keeping the water above freezing without wasting any auxiliary battery power.
The electrolyzer requires standard clean de-ionized (DI) water. Fleets can produce this at their home depots using low-cost reverse-osmosis filtration units for pennies per gallon, completely bypassing the need for multi-million-dollar hydrogen filling stations.
A Class-8 battery-electric truck carries an 8,000 to 10,000 kg battery pack, which severely restricts cargo capacity. SolHydra replaces that deadweight with a lightweight 40 kWh buffer pack, electrolyzer, fuel cell, and water tank, recovering roughly 4,200 kg of additional cargo carrying capability.
Ishan Yadav has filed core patent applications covering the closed-loop direct-feed electrolysis-to-fuel-cell pressure balancing valve, the dynamic solar-coupled predictive energy management system (EMS), and the anti-freeze thermal scavenging architecture.
