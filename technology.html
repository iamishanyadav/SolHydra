ENGINEERING & ARCHITECTURE

How the Tri-Hybrid System Works

SolHydra eliminates heavy 700-bar hydrogen tanks entirely. Instead, ordinary water is converted into clean hydrogen gas on the truck as you drive, using power from rooftop solar panels and a balanced onboard battery.

POWER CONVERSION CYCLE

The Energy Cycle in Plain English

No dangerous pressurized fuel storage. Just a smooth, direct flow of clean energy from water to the wheels.

Solar Harvesting → Energy Buffering → Water Electrolysis → Direct Fuel Cell Feed → Electric Propulsion → Clean Water Exhaust
Step 01 →

Solar Harvesting

Aerodynamic rooftop panels capture clean solar electricity on the move.

Step 02 →

Energy Buffering

A compact onboard battery stores solar power and recovers energy from regenerative braking.

Step 03 →

Water Electrolysis

Clean water is split on demand into hydrogen at safe, ambient pressure.

Step 04 →

Direct Fuel Cell Feed

Hydrogen flows directly into the fuel cell to generate traction electricity without high-pressure storage tanks.

Step 05 →

Electric Propulsion

High-efficiency electric drive motor powers the wheels smoothly and quietly.

Step 06 ✓

Clean Water Exhaust

Pure water vapor is the only emission, with closed-loop recycling back to the water reservoir.

HARDWARE BLUEPRINT

The 6 Core Hardware Subsystems

Every subsystem uses practical, reliable components designed for heavy commercial durability.

Subsystem 01

Rooftop Solar Array

Lightweight, flexible solar panels curved over the truck cab and trailer. Designed to withstand highway winds, vibrations, and weather changes without adding aerodynamic drag.

2.4 kW Peak Power Mono-crystalline Cells IP68 Waterproof
Subsystem 02

Buffer Battery Pack

A safe Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery. Unlike pure electric trucks that carry 8,000 kg of batteries, this right-sized 40 kWh pack acts as an energy buffer for smooth power delivery.

40 kWh Capacity LFP Safe Chemistry Liquid-Cooled
Subsystem 03

Onboard Water Electrolyzer

Uses electricity from the battery to split ordinary water into hydrogen and oxygen gas. Operates below 3 bar pressure (similar to car tires), eliminating explosive hazards.

Pressure: < 3 Bar Zero Explosive Storage Rapid Response
Subsystem 04

Hydrogen Fuel Cell Stack

Takes hydrogen directly from the electrolyzer, combines it with oxygen from the ambient air, and produces steady electrical current with quiet, efficient operation.

Direct-Coupled Feed 60 kW Output Zero CO₂ Emitted
Subsystem 05

Electric Drive Motor

Permanent magnet synchronous motor that delivers instant torque to the truck axles, easily moving full freight loads up steep highway inclines.

Heavy-Duty Torque Regenerative Braking High Efficiency
Subsystem 06

Water Tank & Heating Loop

Stores ordinary clean water in an unpressurized plastic tank. A waste heat loop from the fuel cell warms the tank during winter driving so the water never freezes in cold weather.

Ambient Pressure Tank Anti-Freeze Heated Easy Depo Refilling
